08 · FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about ordering from Chaska Maska.

Ordering & delivery

How do I place an order?

Choose Brockley (SE4) or Peckham (SE15), open that branch's menu page and order online for delivery or collection.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes. Delivery is free within 3 miles of your chosen branch.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Collection is available at both branches.

Which areas do you serve?

Brockley serves Brockley, Ladywell, Lewisham, Honor Oak, Deptford and nearby areas. Peckham serves Peckham, Nunhead, New Cross, Camberwell and nearby areas.

Food & dietary

Do you have vegetarian and vegan dishes?

Yes, we offer a wide range of vegetarian and plant-based dishes, full of flavour.

Do you use artificial colours or additives?

No. Every dish is freshly prepared with quality ingredients and house-ground spices, with no artificial colours or additives.

Offers & dining

How do I get 10% off?

Order directly through our website. The 10% discount applies to website orders.

Can I book a table?

Yes. Dine in is available at our Brockley branch. Use the Book a table button to reserve through the Brockley page.
